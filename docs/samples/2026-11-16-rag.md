# Community Voices: Anki forums, 2026-11-16 to 2026-11-22

_AnkiMobile 26.10 regressions still lead the forum after the 26.10.1 hotfix, as overall activity stays well above the recent average._

The forum had 309 posts in 103 threads by 113 people, against an average of 225 posts in 72 threads by 89 people over the four weeks before. AnkiMobile 26.10 problems were the largest theme again at 33 threads, with the 26.10.1 hotfix out and 26.10.2 in beta [[1]](https://forums.ankiweb.net/t/ankimobile-26-10-1-release/74550/1)[[56]](https://forums.ankiweb.net/t/ankimobile-26-10-26-10-x-beta/71167/37). Desktop crash reports, deck-count confusion, AI and AnkiHub discussion, a long-standing cloze formatting bug and AnkiMobile toolbar design requests made up the rest [[10]](https://forums.ankiweb.net/t/anki-keeps-crashing-help/74617/2)[[19]](https://forums.ankiweb.net/t/syncing-causes-random-cards-to-be-deleted/74570/4)[[32]](https://forums.ankiweb.net/t/feedback-for-ankimobile-ankihub-co/74587/4)[[42]](https://forums.ankiweb.net/t/cloze-ing-completely-formatted-text-un-formats/74660/1)[[47]](https://forums.ankiweb.net/t/suggestion-add-a-toggle-to-restore-the-ankimobile-26-10-liquid-glass-top-bar/74601/1).

## 1. AnkiMobile 26.10 regressions continue

_33 threads · 112 posts · 48 people_

This was the largest theme, with 33 threads, 112 posts and 48 people. The team released 26.10.1 and said a fuller fix for notes-mode suspend indicators would come in 26.10.2 [[1]](https://forums.ankiweb.net/t/ankimobile-26-10-1-release/74550/1). Users still reported cards flashing back to the front on iPhone but not on iPad or desktop [[2]](https://forums.ankiweb.net/t/certain-cards-flashing-back-to-front-after-recent-ankimobile-update/74451/3), and some asked how to undo the update [[3]](https://forums.ankiweb.net/t/undo-26-10-update/74279/109). Not every report held up: a volunteer said one cramming problem did not seem related to the update [[6]](https://forums.ankiweb.net/t/unable-to-cram-untagged-review-cards-in-ankimobile-26-10/74416/9). The theme follows last week's team response and beta call [[9]](https://forums.ankiweb.net/t/anki-team-response-to-feedback-about-ankimobile-26-10/74500/1) and the 26.10 release on 2026-11-03 [[8]](https://forums.ankiweb.net/t/ankimobile-26-10-is-out/74202/1).

> "having the same issue. but it doesnt happen on ipad or desktop....but ya not ideal" ([Certain cards flashing back to front after recent AnkiMobile update](https://forums.ankiweb.net/t/certain-cards-flashing-back-to-front-after-recent-ankimobile-update/74451/3))

> "exactly! I never update anything for this reason. Everything worked why should I update." ([Undo 26.10 update](https://forums.ankiweb.net/t/undo-26-10-update/74279/109))

## 2. Desktop crashes, mostly without debug info

_24 threads · 65 posts · 28 people_

There were 24 threads with 65 posts from 28 people about crashes and errors. One user reported a PanicException in 26.09.x that resembled a crash fixed last year [[12]](https://forums.ankiweb.net/t/anki-crashed-panicexception-assertion-left-right/71457/9). Others reported Anki opening only in safe mode [[13]](https://forums.ankiweb.net/t/anki-crashing-and-need-help/74662/1), and helpers repeatedly asked for debug info and pointed to add-ons and the Troubleshooting Checklist [[10]](https://forums.ankiweb.net/t/anki-keeps-crashing-help/74617/2)[[11]](https://forums.ankiweb.net/t/anki-keeps-crashing-help/74617/4)[[14]](https://forums.ankiweb.net/t/anki-crashing-and-need-help/74662/2). The same pattern of requests for more detail appeared in September threads [[16]](https://forums.ankiweb.net/t/anki-26-09-beta-2/70935/4)[[17]](https://forums.ankiweb.net/t/26-09-performance-issues/71033/44).

> "suddenly today it would not open unless I opened it in safe mode" ([Anki crashing and need help](https://forums.ankiweb.net/t/anki-crashing-and-need-help/74662/1))

> "We'll still need to see that debug info to have any idea why this might be happening." ([Anki keeps crashing! help](https://forums.ankiweb.net/t/anki-keeps-crashing-help/74617/4))

## 3. Deck counters read as missing cards

_17 threads · 55 posts · 19 people_

There were 17 threads with 55 posts from 19 people. Several users thought sync had deleted cards, and helpers explained that Decks-list counters show only cards available for study, not totals [[19]](https://forums.ankiweb.net/t/syncing-causes-random-cards-to-be-deleted/74570/4)[[20]](https://forums.ankiweb.net/t/deck-shows-0-cards-and-re-importing-says-3-001-notes-already-exist/74633/2)[[24]](https://forums.ankiweb.net/t/syncing-causes-random-cards-to-be-deleted/74570/2). One user confirmed that Check Database restored cards but did not update the deck screen [[22]](https://forums.ankiweb.net/t/deck-shows-0-cards-and-re-importing-says-3-001-notes-already-exist/74633/6). Separately, a suggestion thread asked for total note and card counts in the deck overview, and replies pointed to an add-on [[21]](https://forums.ankiweb.net/t/display-the-total-number-of-notes-and-cards-in-the-decks-overview/74593/2)[[23]](https://forums.ankiweb.net/t/display-the-total-number-of-notes-and-cards-in-the-decks-overview/74593/6). Questions about empty decks and missing cards also came up in September and October [[25]](https://forums.ankiweb.net/t/anki-modules-empties/71181/2)[[27]](https://forums.ankiweb.net/t/troubleshooting-no-cards-up-for-review/71612/8).

> "Running "check database" restores the deleted cards but doesn't update the deck screen. Very weird quirk." ([Deck shows 0 cards, and re-importing says "3,001 notes already exist."](https://forums.ankiweb.net/t/deck-shows-0-cards-and-re-importing-says-3-001-notes-already-exist/74633/6))

## 4. AI add-ons and AnkiHub questions

_13 threads · 45 posts · 25 people_

This mixed theme had 13 threads, 45 posts and 25 people. Developers announced AI-built tools and add-ons, one with an explicit AI disclosure [[28]](https://forums.ankiweb.net/t/awesome-ai-resources/55921/6)[[29]](https://forums.ankiweb.net/t/add-on-anki-studio-modern-minimalist-workspace-fsrs-analytics-burnout-shield-1071937836/74537/1)[[30]](https://forums.ankiweb.net/t/add-on-anki-studio-modern-minimalist-workspace-fsrs-analytics-burnout-shield-1071937836/74537/10). In a feedback thread, one user questioned whether AnkiHub's direction matches the founder's vision, linking it to anger over the 26.10 UI changes [[32]](https://forums.ankiweb.net/t/feedback-for-ankimobile-ankihub-co/74587/4). A ChatGPT-on-iOS request from last week continued, and the poster decided to wait for a simpler setup [[31]](https://forums.ankiweb.net/t/official-anki-plugin-for-chatgpt-on-ios/74256/5)[[34]](https://forums.ankiweb.net/t/official-anki-plugin-for-chatgpt-on-ios/74256/4).

> "IMO it is unclear whether AnkiHub is developing Anki in line with the founder’s intended vision or not." ([Feedback for AnkiMobile + AnkiHub & Co](https://forums.ankiweb.net/t/feedback-for-ankimobile-ankihub-co/74587/4))

## 5. Cloze markers strip formatting; F7 color bug

_4 threads · 12 posts · 6 people_

This was a small theme, with 4 threads, 12 posts and 6 people. A user described in detail how clozing fully formatted text removes the formatting [[42]](https://forums.ankiweb.net/t/cloze-ing-completely-formatted-text-un-formats/74660/1), and the post was merged into an older thread that links to an open GitHub issue and earlier failed fix attempts [[39]](https://forums.ankiweb.net/t/adding-a-cloze-marker-removes-formatting/48889/6)[[41]](https://forums.ankiweb.net/t/adding-a-cloze-marker-removes-formatting/48889/6). This is recurring: in September a user said the problem was worse in 26.09.2 [[44]](https://forums.ankiweb.net/t/adding-a-cloze-marker-removes-formatting/48889/4). One Mac user reported that F7 always applies blue text color [[37]](https://forums.ankiweb.net/t/on-mac-f7-changes-text-color-only-to-blue-in-edit-and-browse-windows/74661/3)[[38]](https://forums.ankiweb.net/t/on-mac-f7-changes-text-color-only-to-blue-in-edit-and-browse-windows/74661/1).

> "No matter what color I select, pressing F7 turns selected text to blue. This error does not occur when clicking the Text Color icon." ([On Mac, F7 changes text color only to blue in Edit and Browse windows](https://forums.ankiweb.net/t/on-mac-f7-changes-text-color-only-to-blue-in-edit-and-browse-windows/74661/3))

> "On desktop, cloze-ing completely formatted text un-formats it." ([Cloze-ing completely formatted text un-formats](https://forums.ankiweb.net/t/cloze-ing-completely-formatted-text-un-formats/74660/1))

## 6. Some users want the Liquid Glass bar back

_5 threads · 11 posts · 10 people_

There were 5 threads with 11 posts from 10 people. After 26.10.1 replaced the floating capsule bar with a slim flat bar and removed the darker bar styles [[50]](https://forums.ankiweb.net/t/ankimobile-26-10-1-release/74550/1), one user asked for a toggle to restore the 26.10 Liquid Glass design [[47]](https://forums.ankiweb.net/t/suggestion-add-a-toggle-to-restore-the-ankimobile-26-10-liquid-glass-top-bar/74601/1), and another supported adding the option [[46]](https://forums.ankiweb.net/t/suggestion-add-a-toggle-to-restore-the-ankimobile-26-10-liquid-glass-top-bar/74601/2). On iPad, users reported clipped toolbar buttons, the top bar overlapping the window controls, and blue options that are hard to tap [[48]](https://forums.ankiweb.net/t/make-use-of-the-ipados-toolbar-in-ankimobile/74626/2)[[49]](https://forums.ankiweb.net/t/ankimobile-26-10-26-10-x-beta/71167/35). Last week's reports were about toolbar gaps and unreadable dark bar text [[52]](https://forums.ankiweb.net/t/ankimobile-26-10-26-10-x-beta/71167/28)[[54]](https://forums.ankiweb.net/t/the-style-of-the-review-interface-top-bar/74241/5).

> "I personally much preferred the 26.10 design. I think it looked more modern and more consistent with the design of other apps." ([Suggestion: Add a toggle to restore the AnkiMobile 26.10 Liquid Glass top bar](https://forums.ankiweb.net/t/suggestion-add-a-toggle-to-restore-the-ankimobile-26-10-liquid-glass-top-bar/74601/1))

> "The new top bar overlaps with the window’s traffic icons." ([AnkiMobile 26.10 - 26.10.x Beta](https://forums.ankiweb.net/t/ankimobile-26-10-26-10-x-beta/71167/35))
