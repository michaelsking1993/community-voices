# Community Voices: Anki forums, 2026-11-16 to 2026-11-22

_AnkiMobile 26.10 regressions still led the forum after the 26.10.1 hotfix, while desktop crash reports and missing-card worries also grew._

Activity was well above recent weeks: 309 posts in 103 threads by 113 people, against an average of 225 posts in 72 threads by 89 people over the 4 weeks before. AnkiMobile 26.10 problems were again the largest theme (33 threads, 112 posts, 48 people), though the trend count fell from 76 to 39 after the 26.10.1 hotfix shipped [[10]](https://forums.ankiweb.net/t/ankimobile-26-10-1-release/74550/1). Desktop crashes, deck-count confusion and AI tools made up most of the rest, and an Anki 26.11 beta and a 26.10.2 AnkiMobile beta drew early bug reports [[64]](https://forums.ankiweb.net/t/anki-26-11-beta-1/74435/2)[[65]](https://forums.ankiweb.net/t/ankimobile-26-10-26-10-x-beta/71167/37).

## 1. AnkiMobile 26.10 fallout continues past the hotfix

_33 threads · 112 posts · 48 people_

The 26.10.1 hotfix arrived on 2026-11-16. It flattened the review top bar and restored suspend/unsuspend in Notes mode, and the team said 26.10.2 would follow [[10]](https://forums.ankiweb.net/t/ankimobile-26-10-1-release/74550/1)[[1]](https://forums.ankiweb.net/t/ankimobile-26-10-1-release/74550/1). New regressions were still reported: cloze cards with many clozes flash back to the question side on iPhone [[12]](https://forums.ankiweb.net/t/certain-cards-flashing-back-to-front-after-recent-ankimobile-update/74451/2)[[2]](https://forums.ankiweb.net/t/certain-cards-flashing-back-to-front-after-recent-ankimobile-update/74451/3), Save and Cancel disappear from the iPad Add screen [[13]](https://forums.ankiweb.net/t/save-and-cancel-button-gone-on-add-note-screen-ipad/74606/3), and hardware-keyboard events still fail to reach card JavaScript [[8]](https://forums.ankiweb.net/t/ankimobile-26-10-key-events-from-a-hardware-keyboard-no-longer-reach-card-javascript-during-review/74494/14). Several posts went beyond bugs to loss of trust and the change in stewardship, and one user asked how to undo the update [[9]](https://forums.ankiweb.net/t/undo-26-10-update/74279/117)[[7]](https://forums.ankiweb.net/t/feedback-for-ankimobile-ankihub-co/74587/1)[[3]](https://forums.ankiweb.net/t/undo-26-10-update/74279/109). Not every report was a regression: one cramming problem was judged unrelated to the update [[6]](https://forums.ankiweb.net/t/unable-to-cram-untagged-review-cards-in-ankimobile-26-10/74416/9).

> "Then, the first update fixed some of the worst issues for me." ([Undo 26.10 update](https://forums.ankiweb.net/t/undo-26-10-update/74279/117))

> "The argument of "old-timers don't like change" does not really work here. The update was dysfunctional, not unpalatable." ([Feedback for AnkiMobile + AnkiHub & Co](https://forums.ankiweb.net/t/feedback-for-ankimobile-ankihub-co/74587/1))

## 2. Desktop crashes, mostly without debug info

_24 threads · 65 posts · 28 people_

Several threads reported desktop Anki crashing or failing to open. Replies asked for debug info and pointed to the Troubleshooting Checklist and add-ons as a likely cause [[17]](https://forums.ankiweb.net/t/anki-keeps-crashing-help/74617/2)[[18]](https://forums.ankiweb.net/t/anki-keeps-crashing-help/74617/4)[[20]](https://forums.ankiweb.net/t/anki-crashing-and-need-help/74662/1)[[21]](https://forums.ankiweb.net/t/anki-crashing-and-need-help/74662/2). One report was a PanicException on 26.09.x that resembles an issue fixed last year [[19]](https://forums.ankiweb.net/t/anki-crashed-panicexception-assertion-left-right/71457/9). Other threads in this group were not crashes: a request to bump the bundled Qt to 6.12.2 for a macOS accessibility crash bug [[25]](https://forums.ankiweb.net/t/update-ankis-6-11-2-bundled-qt-version-to-the-6-12-2-version/74561/1), an editor down-arrow bug that reproduces with add-ons disabled [[27]](https://forums.ankiweb.net/t/down-arrow-gets-stuck-on-empty-lines-in-the-add-screen-editor/74616/1), and a Persian translation not showing [[23]](https://forums.ankiweb.net/t/help-me-show-anki-in-my-language-persian/74394/4)[[24]](https://forums.ankiweb.net/t/help-me-show-anki-in-my-language-persian/74394/5). Crash threads have come up regularly since the 26.09 cycle [[28]](https://forums.ankiweb.net/t/anki-26-09-beta-2/70935/4)[[29]](https://forums.ankiweb.net/t/26-09-performance-issues/71033/44)[[30]](https://forums.ankiweb.net/t/anki-crashing-on-startup/71105/1).

> "The current version has a known macOS accessibility bug (QTBUG-149612) that can cause Anki to crash when other applications access its interface." ([Update Anki's 6.11.2 bundled Qt version to the 6.12.2 version](https://forums.ankiweb.net/t/update-ankis-6-11-2-bundled-qt-version-to-the-6-12-2-version/74561/1))

> "the cursor stops where the empty line is and won't go down any further. Left or Right arrow gets past it." ([Down arrow gets stuck on empty lines in the Add screen editor](https://forums.ankiweb.net/t/down-arrow-gets-stuck-on-empty-lines-in-the-add-screen-editor/74616/1))

## 3. Missing cards that are usually counter confusion

_17 threads · 55 posts · 19 people_

Users reported cards vanishing after sync or decks showing 0 cards. Helpers explained that Decks-list counters show only cards available for study today, not totals [[31]](https://forums.ankiweb.net/t/syncing-causes-random-cards-to-be-deleted/74570/4)[[32]](https://forums.ankiweb.net/t/deck-shows-0-cards-and-re-importing-says-3-001-notes-already-exist/74633/2)[[36]](https://forums.ankiweb.net/t/syncing-causes-random-cards-to-be-deleted/74570/2). In one AnkiMobile case, Check Database restored deleted cards but the deck screen did not update, which a user called a quirk [[34]](https://forums.ankiweb.net/t/deck-shows-0-cards-and-re-importing-says-3-001-notes-already-exist/74633/6). A suggestion to show total note and card counts in the deck overview was pointed to an add-on [[33]](https://forums.ankiweb.net/t/display-the-total-number-of-notes-and-cards-in-the-decks-overview/74593/2)[[35]](https://forums.ankiweb.net/t/display-the-total-number-of-notes-and-cards-in-the-decks-overview/74593/6). The same confusion appeared in earlier weeks [[37]](https://forums.ankiweb.net/t/anki-modules-empties/71181/2)[[39]](https://forums.ankiweb.net/t/troubleshooting-no-cards-up-for-review/71612/8), and this week's trend count (30) is the highest of the six weeks.

> "Running "check database" restores the deleted cards but doesn't update the deck screen. Very weird quirk." ([Deck shows 0 cards, and re-importing says "3,001 notes already exist."](https://forums.ankiweb.net/t/deck-shows-0-cards-and-re-importing-says-3-001-notes-already-exist/74633/6))

## 4. AI-built tools and AnkiHub stewardship

_13 threads · 45 posts · 25 people_

Developers shared AI-assisted projects, including the AnkiNote web app and the Anki Studio add-on, which disclosed that most of its code was written with AI [[40]](https://forums.ankiweb.net/t/awesome-ai-resources/55921/6)[[41]](https://forums.ankiweb.net/t/add-on-anki-studio-modern-minimalist-workspace-fsrs-analytics-burnout-shield-1071937836/74537/1)[[42]](https://forums.ankiweb.net/t/add-on-anki-studio-modern-minimalist-workspace-fsrs-analytics-burnout-shield-1071937836/74537/10). One user decided not to set up a ChatGPT integration on iOS without a Mac running, following a setup guide posted the week before [[43]](https://forums.ankiweb.net/t/official-anki-plugin-for-chatgpt-on-ios/74256/5)[[46]](https://forums.ankiweb.net/t/official-anki-plugin-for-chatgpt-on-ios/74256/4). In the AnkiMobile feedback thread, users debated whether AnkiHub's development matches the founder's vision [[44]](https://forums.ankiweb.net/t/feedback-for-ankimobile-ankihub-co/74587/4). In another thread, one person argued that UX around scheduling matters more than new scheduling models [[45]](https://forums.ankiweb.net/t/suggestion-to-damien-migrating-to-an-nn-neural-net/60830/72).

> "IMO it is unclear whether AnkiHub is developing Anki in line with the founder’s intended vision or not." ([Feedback for AnkiMobile + AnkiHub & Co](https://forums.ankiweb.net/t/feedback-for-ankimobile-ankihub-co/74587/4))

## 5. Small editor bugs: F7 colour and cloze formatting

_4 threads · 12 posts · 6 people_

This theme is small (4 threads, 6 people) and new by trend count. One Mac user found that F7 always applies blue regardless of the chosen colour, while the toolbar button works [[49]](https://forums.ankiweb.net/t/on-mac-f7-changes-text-color-only-to-blue-in-edit-and-browse-windows/74661/3)[[50]](https://forums.ankiweb.net/t/on-mac-f7-changes-text-color-only-to-blue-in-edit-and-browse-windows/74661/1). The long-standing problem of cloze markers stripping formatting resurfaced, with links to earlier developer comments and an open GitHub issue [[51]](https://forums.ankiweb.net/t/adding-a-cloze-marker-removes-formatting/48889/6)[[54]](https://forums.ankiweb.net/t/adding-a-cloze-marker-removes-formatting/48889/4). A workaround was posted for having TTS read image alt text [[52]](https://forums.ankiweb.net/t/tts-should-read-the-alt-tag-of-img/74613/3).

> "no matter what color I choose to format text, pressing F7 only changes highlighted text to blue, in both Edit and Browse windows." ([On Mac, F7 changes text color only to blue in Edit and Browse windows](https://forums.ankiweb.net/t/on-mac-f7-changes-text-color-only-to-blue-in-edit-and-browse-windows/74661/1))

## 6. Split opinions on the AnkiMobile top bar

_5 threads · 11 posts · 10 people_

After 26.10.1 replaced the capsule top bar and removed the darker bar styles [[10]](https://forums.ankiweb.net/t/ankimobile-26-10-1-release/74550/1), one user asked for a toggle to bring back the 26.10 Liquid Glass design [[57]](https://forums.ankiweb.net/t/suggestion-add-a-toggle-to-restore-the-ankimobile-26-10-liquid-glass-top-bar/74601/1). Another, who prefers the new bar, still supported having the option [[56]](https://forums.ankiweb.net/t/suggestion-add-a-toggle-to-restore-the-ankimobile-26-10-liquid-glass-top-bar/74601/2). Other posts covered hard-to-tap iPad toolbar buttons and mismatched toolbar colours [[58]](https://forums.ankiweb.net/t/make-use-of-the-ipados-toolbar-in-ankimobile/74626/2)[[59]](https://forums.ankiweb.net/t/why-different-background-colors-for-the-top-and-bottom-toolbars/74599/3). On AnkiDroid, a request to save drawings as editable SVG was welcomed but limited by volunteer capacity [[60]](https://forums.ankiweb.net/t/save-built-in-drawings-as-editable-vectors-svg-so-they-can-be-re-edited-later/74659/2).

> "I know that a lot of people were upset about the top bar design introduced in AnkiMobile 26.10, but I really liked it." ([Suggestion: Add a toggle to restore the AnkiMobile 26.10 Liquid Glass top bar](https://forums.ankiweb.net/t/suggestion-add-a-toggle-to-restore-the-ankimobile-26-10-liquid-glass-top-bar/74601/1))
