# Community Voices: Anki forums, 2026-11-16 to 2026-11-22

_Forum activity rose well above the recent average as iPad users reported the Add screen's Save and Cancel bar vanishing after AnkiMobile 26.10.1, and complaints about other 26.10.x changes and AnkiHub's direction continued._

The forum had 309 posts in 103 threads by 113 people, up from a four-week average of 225 posts in 72 threads by 89 people. Most of the volume was about AnkiMobile 26.10 and the 26.10.1 hotfix [[299]](https://forums.ankiweb.net/t/ankimobile-26-10-1-release/74550/1): a top bar that disappears while adding notes, removed bar styles, keyboard regressions, and a long thread questioning AnkiHub's stewardship and use of AI [[26]](https://forums.ankiweb.net/t/save-and-cancel-button-gone-on-add-note-screen-ipad/74606/14)[[93]](https://forums.ankiweb.net/t/feedback-for-ankimobile-ankihub-co/74587/1). Desktop discussion was smaller and included a bug found in the 26.11 beta, an add-note crash, and long-standing editor quirks [[5]](https://forums.ankiweb.net/t/anki-26-11-beta-1/74435/2)[[211]](https://forums.ankiweb.net/t/anki-crashed-panicexception-assertion-left-right/71457/8)[[68]](https://forums.ankiweb.net/t/adding-a-cloze-marker-removes-formatting/48889/5).

## 1. iPad Add screen loses Save/Cancel

One thread collected about a dozen reports of the top bar with Save and Cancel disappearing on the Add screen, which leaves users unable to save new notes [[14]](https://forums.ankiweb.net/t/save-and-cancel-button-gone-on-add-note-screen-ipad/74606/1)[[15]](https://forums.ankiweb.net/t/save-and-cancel-button-gone-on-add-note-screen-ipad/74606/3)[[17]](https://forums.ankiweb.net/t/save-and-cancel-button-gone-on-add-note-screen-ipad/74606/5)[[18]](https://forums.ankiweb.net/t/save-and-cancel-button-gone-on-add-note-screen-ipad/74606/6). Most reporters said it started with the 26.10.1 patch, it happened with both bar styles, and one person said it happens especially when pasting images from Goodnotes [[20]](https://forums.ankiweb.net/t/save-and-cancel-button-gone-on-add-note-screen-ipad/74606/8)[[21]](https://forums.ankiweb.net/t/save-and-cancel-button-gone-on-add-note-screen-ipad/74606/9)[[22]](https://forums.ankiweb.net/t/save-and-cancel-button-gone-on-add-note-screen-ipad/74606/10)[[24]](https://forums.ankiweb.net/t/save-and-cancel-button-gone-on-add-note-screen-ipad/74606/12). The reports were mixed in two ways: one user saw it on an iPhone, and another said it has happened since 25.09 [[17]](https://forums.ankiweb.net/t/save-and-cancel-button-gone-on-add-note-screen-ipad/74606/5)[[23]](https://forums.ankiweb.net/t/save-and-cancel-button-gone-on-add-note-screen-ipad/74606/11)[[26]](https://forums.ankiweb.net/t/save-and-cancel-button-gone-on-add-note-screen-ipad/74606/14). Similar reports appeared in Image Occlusion and in separate threads [[193]](https://forums.ankiweb.net/t/top-bar-randomly-disappearing-in-image-occlusion-can-t-save-progress/67901/7)[[202]](https://forums.ankiweb.net/t/make-use-of-the-ipados-toolbar-in-ankimobile/74626/1)[[258]](https://forums.ankiweb.net/t/add-button-on-image-occlusions-randomly-disappearing/74584/1). The team and a volunteer could not reproduce it and suggested a TestFlight route back to 26.10 [[26]](https://forums.ankiweb.net/t/save-and-cancel-button-gone-on-add-note-screen-ipad/74606/14)[[27]](https://forums.ankiweb.net/t/save-and-cancel-button-gone-on-add-note-screen-ipad/74606/15).

> "The top bar containing the “Save” and “Cancel” buttons randomly disappears after just a few minutes, even while I am actively creating and typing a new card." ([Save and cancel button gone on Add note screen - iPad](https://forums.ankiweb.net/t/save-and-cancel-button-gone-on-add-note-screen-ipad/74606/3))

> "We just haven't been able to identify *why* it is happening -- or why it is happening to only some users, on only some devices, etc." ([Save and cancel button gone on Add note screen - iPad](https://forums.ankiweb.net/t/save-and-cancel-button-gone-on-add-note-screen-ipad/74606/14))

## 2. Other 26.10.x regressions and design complaints

The 26.10.1 hotfix flattened the review top bar and removed the Slate, Dark and Black bar styles [[299]](https://forums.ankiweb.net/t/ankimobile-26-10-1-release/74550/1). Removing those styles brought back hardware-keyboard scrolling and remote problems for people who had used Black as a workaround [[123]](https://forums.ankiweb.net/t/ankimobile-26-10-key-events-from-a-hardware-keyboard-no-longer-reach-card-javascript-during-review/74494/13)[[124]](https://forums.ankiweb.net/t/ankimobile-26-10-key-events-from-a-hardware-keyboard-no-longer-reach-card-javascript-during-review/74494/14)[[300]](https://forums.ankiweb.net/t/external-keyboard-review-scroll-issue/74548/1). Some users asked for the black bar back, one asked for a toggle to restore the 26.10 Liquid Glass bar, and others objected to a new drop shadow and to mismatched toolbar colors [[238]](https://forums.ankiweb.net/t/dark-theme-is-gone-now-the-top-bar-is-gray/74595/1)[[204]](https://forums.ankiweb.net/t/suggestion-add-a-toggle-to-restore-the-ankimobile-26-10-liquid-glass-top-bar/74601/1)[[191]](https://forums.ankiweb.net/t/remove-the-drop-shadow-under-the-top-bar/74592/1)[[32]](https://forums.ankiweb.net/t/why-different-background-colors-for-the-top-and-bottom-toolbars/74599/1). Smaller reports covered the bottom bar that can no longer be hidden, swipe actions that do not fire, review counts and notifications that only update after a sync, a black flash on cards with many clozes, buttons cut off on the iPhone Duo's outer screen, and missing sort-by-ID and sort-by-created options in Browse [[250]](https://forums.ankiweb.net/t/the-bottom-bar-can-no-longer-be-hidden/74259/6)[[251]](https://forums.ankiweb.net/t/the-bottom-bar-can-no-longer-be-hidden/74259/7)[[13]](https://forums.ankiweb.net/t/ankimobile-26-10-26-10-x-beta/71167/37)[[182]](https://forums.ankiweb.net/t/26-10-1-notifications-not-working-in-the-background-unless-i-open-app-first/74629/1)[[184]](https://forums.ankiweb.net/t/26-10-1-notifications-not-working-in-the-background-unless-i-open-app-first/74629/3)[[115]](https://forums.ankiweb.net/t/undo-26-10-update/74279/113)[[269]](https://forums.ankiweb.net/t/certain-cards-flashing-back-to-front-after-recent-ankimobile-update/74451/2)[[277]](https://forums.ankiweb.net/t/iphone-revealing-answer-on-cloze-heavy-cards-causes-black-flash-and-resets-to-question-side/74573/1)[[281]](https://forums.ankiweb.net/t/buttons-are-partially-hidden-in-26-10-1-duo/74568/1)[[110]](https://forums.ankiweb.net/t/undo-26-10-update/74279/108)[[248]](https://forums.ankiweb.net/t/26-10-cannot-sort-by-card-created-date/74317/2).

> "Now with 26.10.1, The option of bar Colors was removed and the scrolling issue came again" ([AnkiMobile 26.10: key events from a hardware keyboard no longer reach card JavaScript during review](https://forums.ankiweb.net/t/ankimobile-26-10-key-events-from-a-hardware-keyboard-no-longer-reach-card-javascript-during-review/74494/13))

> "Imagine if a web browser placed a drop shadow at the top of every website. It would be ridiculous" ([Remove the drop shadow under the top bar](https://forums.ankiweb.net/t/remove-the-drop-shadow-under-the-top-bar/74592/1))

## 3. Trust in AnkiHub and arguments over AI

A long post from a user since 2008 argued that the AnkiMobile release reflected too much AI and predicted Anki Desktop could become non-FOSS. That post drew a multi-day thread split between agreement, pushback, and an argument over whether the post itself was AI-written [[93]](https://forums.ankiweb.net/t/feedback-for-ankimobile-ankihub-co/74587/1)[[94]](https://forums.ankiweb.net/t/feedback-for-ankimobile-ankihub-co/74587/2)[[95]](https://forums.ankiweb.net/t/feedback-for-ankimobile-ankihub-co/74587/3)[[97]](https://forums.ankiweb.net/t/feedback-for-ankimobile-ankihub-co/74587/5)[[98]](https://forums.ankiweb.net/t/feedback-for-ankimobile-ankihub-co/74587/6)[[100]](https://forums.ankiweb.net/t/feedback-for-ankimobile-ankihub-co/74587/8). Other users asked whether they can stay on old versions and still sync, or maintain their own copy, and said they fear what comes next [[29]](https://forums.ankiweb.net/t/rollback-is-not-practical/74679/3)[[31]](https://forums.ankiweb.net/t/rollback-is-not-practical/74679/1)[[119]](https://forums.ankiweb.net/t/undo-26-10-update/74279/117). One user asked for the FAQs to be updated, since they still describe funding in Damien's voice [[255]](https://forums.ankiweb.net/t/update-funding-and-ankidroid-comparisons-in-the-faqs/74590/1). AI also came up around add-ons: a large AI-built UI add-on added a disclosure after users asked, and in a scheduler thread users questioned AI-driven forks [[128]](https://forums.ankiweb.net/t/add-on-anki-studio-modern-minimalist-workspace-fsrs-analytics-burnout-shield-1071937836/74537/3)[[131]](https://forums.ankiweb.net/t/add-on-anki-studio-modern-minimalist-workspace-fsrs-analytics-burnout-shield-1071937836/74537/7)[[133]](https://forums.ankiweb.net/t/add-on-anki-studio-modern-minimalist-workspace-fsrs-analytics-burnout-shield-1071937836/74537/9)[[216]](https://forums.ankiweb.net/t/suggestion-to-damien-migrating-to-an-nn-neural-net/60830/71)[[218]](https://forums.ankiweb.net/t/suggestion-to-damien-migrating-to-an-nn-neural-net/60830/73).

> "But that’s exactly why my biggest fear now is: what comes next? I’m afraid that’s what they call a loss of trust..." ([Undo 26.10 update](https://forums.ankiweb.net/t/undo-26-10-update/74279/117))

> "I am not hating on the developers, especially not the ones that have been contributing good code for long" ([Feedback for AnkiMobile + AnkiHub & Co](https://forums.ankiweb.net/t/feedback-for-ankimobile-ankihub-co/74587/1))

## 4. Feared data loss that was mostly sync or counter confusion

Several threads reported deleted cards, missing decks, or repeating reviews [[146]](https://forums.ankiweb.net/t/syncing-causes-random-cards-to-be-deleted/74570/1)[[154]](https://forums.ankiweb.net/t/seeing-zero-new-cards-despite-troubleshooting-and-now-no-audio-too/74628/1)[[163]](https://forums.ankiweb.net/t/deck-shows-0-cards-and-re-importing-says-3-001-notes-already-exist/74633/1)[[144]](https://forums.ankiweb.net/t/missing-deck-in-ankidroid-and-anki-web/74640/1). In most of them, the replies pointed to sync conflicts, deck counters that show only cards available today, suspended cards, or a different AnkiWeb account [[151]](https://forums.ankiweb.net/t/syncing-causes-random-cards-to-be-deleted/74570/6)[[160]](https://forums.ankiweb.net/t/seeing-zero-new-cards-despite-troubleshooting-and-now-no-audio-too/74628/7)[[164]](https://forums.ankiweb.net/t/deck-shows-0-cards-and-re-importing-says-3-001-notes-already-exist/74633/2)[[170]](https://forums.ankiweb.net/t/deck-shows-0-cards-and-re-importing-says-3-001-notes-already-exist/74633/8)[[264]](https://forums.ankiweb.net/t/loss-of-work-and-need-help-recovering/74574/2). Two users had genuinely lost data: one person's decks were removed for inactivity with no backups left, and another person's iPad had offloaded AnkiMobile before most decks had synced [[153]](https://forums.ankiweb.net/t/request-to-restore-deleted-decks-after-period-of-inactivity-on-the-app-and-the-anki-web/74637/2)[[263]](https://forums.ankiweb.net/t/loss-of-work-and-need-help-recovering/74574/1). A German learner traced missing ß audio files to iOS treating filenames case-sensitively [[74]](https://forums.ankiweb.net/t/files-including-the-german-ss-in-its-name-not-recognised-in-ankimobile/74654/5)[[75]](https://forums.ankiweb.net/t/files-including-the-german-ss-in-its-name-not-recognised-in-ankimobile/74654/6).

> "Anki simply doesn't have the ability to randomly delete notes during sync, unless you've already deleted them yourself from your collection." ([Syncing causes random cards to be deleted](https://forums.ankiweb.net/t/syncing-causes-random-cards-to-be-deleted/74570/6))

> "Those decks were over a year of work I made myself for my exams, and losing them is really upsetting." ([Loss of work and need help recovering](https://forums.ankiweb.net/t/loss-of-work-and-need-help-recovering/74574/1))

## 5. Desktop: 26.11 beta and editor bugs

A beta tester found that filtered decks sorted by Relative overdueness fail in 26.11b1 with an ambiguous column error, traced it to a specific commit, and planned a draft PR [[5]](https://forums.ankiweb.net/t/anki-26-11-beta-1/74435/2). A moderator also pointed a user blocked by Windows Smart App Control to the beta [[257]](https://forums.ankiweb.net/t/ask-for-fix-error-message-on-anki/74589/2). On 26.09.4, one user reported a crash when adding notes, posted in two threads, that also occurs in safe mode [[211]](https://forums.ankiweb.net/t/anki-crashed-panicexception-assertion-left-right/71457/8)[[215]](https://forums.ankiweb.net/t/debug-info-error-message/74611/1)[[212]](https://forums.ankiweb.net/t/anki-crashed-panicexception-assertion-left-right/71457/9). Long-standing editor issues also came up: clozing fully formatted text removes its formatting (an old open issue), and the down arrow gets stuck on empty lines, which two people confirmed [[68]](https://forums.ankiweb.net/t/adding-a-cloze-marker-removes-formatting/48889/5)[[69]](https://forums.ankiweb.net/t/adding-a-cloze-marker-removes-formatting/48889/6)[[42]](https://forums.ankiweb.net/t/down-arrow-gets-stuck-on-empty-lines-in-the-add-screen-editor/74616/1)[[47]](https://forums.ankiweb.net/t/down-arrow-gets-stuck-on-empty-lines-in-the-add-screen-editor/74616/6). One user also proposed a workaround for a macOS crash when other apps access Browse through accessibility tools [[278]](https://forums.ankiweb.net/t/macos-accessibility-scan-can-crash-browse-when-a-row-is-selected/70882/7)[[280]](https://forums.ankiweb.net/t/macos-accessibility-scan-can-crash-browse-when-a-row-is-selected/70882/9).

> "Building or rebuilding a filtered deck sorted by `Relative overdueness` fails with `ambiguous column name: data`." ([Anki 26.11 Beta 1](https://forums.ankiweb.net/t/anki-26-11-beta-1/74435/2))

> "This happens every time after I make a few flashcards, and I have to close Anki all the time." ([Anki crashed - PanicException: assertion left == right](https://forums.ankiweb.net/t/anki-crashed-panicexception-assertion-left-right/71457/8))
