## 1. FSRS questions probably continued

This is a guess, but questions about FSRS (the Free Spaced Repetition Scheduler) have been among the most common forum topics since it became part of Anki. Likely threads include how to choose a desired retention, when to re-optimize parameters, and why intervals changed after optimizing. Users moving over from the older SM-2 scheduler also tend to ask whether their review history carries over correctly.

## 2. Sync and cross-platform problems as a likely constant

We expect, without evidence for this specific week, the usual stream of reports about sync conflicts, full-sync prompts and media that does not sync. These often involve mixing desktop, AnkiMobile and AnkiDroid, or running different versions on each device. These threads are usually support requests rather than signs of a new bug, but a sudden cluster would be worth watching.

## 3. Exam-season pressure from students

Mid-November is close to end-of-term exams for many university and medical students, and the JLPT is usually held on the first Sunday of December. In past years this period has brought more posts about review backlogs, rescheduling or postponing cards, and filtered decks for cramming. This is a seasonal assumption, not an observation from this week.

## 4. Add-on compatibility and note templates

Add-on breakage after desktop updates is a recurring topic, as are questions about card templates, CSS styling, cloze notes and image occlusion. Whether any of this spiked this week depends on whether a release shipped recently, which we cannot confirm. Template and styling questions usually stay steady regardless of releases.
